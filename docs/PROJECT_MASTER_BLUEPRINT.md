# Project Master Blueprint
Version 1.0 — Approved product foundation — 2026-11-04

## Vision
Build a reliable, high-quality programming education platform that makes practical software-development knowledge accessible through six teaching languages while maintaining consistent technical accuracy and learning outcomes.

## Audience
Absolute beginners, school students, college students, job seekers, working professionals, self-learners, and career switchers.

## Platforms
1. Responsive website first.
2. Progressive Web App.
3. Android application.
4. iOS in the future.

## Languages
Telugu, English, Malayalam, Kannada, Tamil, Hindi. Language parity is a product requirement.

## Learning outcomes
Understand concepts; read, explain, write, and debug code; complete practical projects; demonstrate skills through assessments; progress toward career readiness.

## Curriculum
Use W3Schools public curriculum categories and topic taxonomy to discover coverage. Do not reproduce protected lesson prose, examples, diagrams, or distinctive page layouts. Create original lessons, code samples, visuals, exercises, quizzes, and projects. Track topic source, coverage, level, prerequisites, and review state.

## Levels
Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional. Detailed mapping and prerequisites belong to curriculum planning.

## Lesson model
Learning objectives; prerequisites; native-language explanation; terminology; original code examples and expected output; step-by-step walkthrough; common mistakes; practice; knowledge checks; practical challenge.

## Planned capabilities
Course catalogue and learning paths; modules/chapters/topics/lessons; search and glossary; localized examples; exercises/quizzes/projects/assessments; progress and achievements; certificates; student dashboard; content administration; AI tutor; isolated code playground; AST-based code analyzer.

## Commercial model — approved direction
Adopt a freemium model: the website and Android app are free to access/download, with an optional low-cost Premium subscription. Keep core learning access available on the Free tier; reserve higher usage quotas and selected advanced capabilities for Premium. An institutional plan may be evaluated later.
- Initial price hypotheses for validation only: ₹49 monthly, ₹129 quarterly, ₹399 annually. These are not launch prices and require cost, market, tax, payment-policy and user research before approval.
- Candidate Premium benefits: expanded learning paths and practice access, higher isolated code-execution quotas, enhanced projects/assessment features, AI Tutor quota and Code Structure Analyzer access. Exact feature entitlements and limits remain to be designed.
- Free and Premium quotas must be server-enforced, abuse-resistant and observable. Apply per-user and global budgets, rate limits, execution time/resource ceilings, AI spend caps, alerts and kill switches before enabling paid or costly services.
- Do not promise unlimited AI or code execution. Measure actual per-active-user costs and contribution margin before setting final prices.
- Android app download remains free; evaluate Google Play Billing and any permitted alternative billing/program rules for the launch market at implementation time. No payment provider or launch date is approved.
- Subscription and payment implementation belongs to R (Monetization) and must pass E10 payment-security, E14 testing and E15 approval gates.

## Quality and security
Least privilege, server-side authorization, schema/input validation, privacy minimization, accessible UI, automated unit/integration/E2E/regression/security/performance tests, monitoring, backups, recovery, rollback.

## Success measures
Curriculum coverage; six-language parity; technical and native-language review quality; learner completion and project outcomes; AI tutor accuracy/safety/latency/cost; sandbox isolation; availability, performance, accessibility, privacy, recovery, free-to-premium conversion, retention and contribution margin.

## Constraints
Self-development with AI assistance; avoid unnecessary outsourcing. Prefer open standards and free/open-source or low-cost development tiers. Hosting, AI, storage, and execution may still incur costs. Defer domain purchase until launch readiness.

## Approval boundaries
Product foundation is approved. The freemium direction is approved; price points, detailed entitlements, payment provider, billing implementation and launch date are provisional/pending their dedicated phases. Stack, architecture, detailed curriculum, threat model, UI identity, and release criteria remain to be designed and reviewed in their respective phases.
